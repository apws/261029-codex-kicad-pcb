== Purpose

Create a usable Codex-only starting point for automated KiCad work on our 7xsys/7xmod/7xmem designs. JLCPCB is our preferred manufacturer. We have previously tried Java Freerouting briefly; this laboratory should establish whether a better organized Codex-controlled loop improves usefulness, rather than assume a different router is better.

The user reports discovering upstream through a JLCPCB Twitter/X share. The post has not been independently identified. This explains its relevance to our manufacturing workflow; it is not evidence of electrical correctness or routing quality.

== Intended use

. Choose one complete native KiCad design directory and a precise task.
. Create a new experiment using `tools/pcb-lab.py init`; keep the source recoverable.
. Fill in the board brief: interfaces, voltage/current, mechanics, layers, critical nets and manufacturing requirements. Unknowns stay unknown.
. Establish ERC/DRC/netlist and geometric baseline.
. Try one operation: readback, footprint movement, schematic edit, or DSN/SES routing.
. Recheck and visually inspect; record evidence and limitations in the experiment report.
. Promote an accepted design to a fixed manufacturing package only after design review.

The GNSS example is optional background and a file-processing fixture. Its RF rules, dimensions, stackup and parts are never defaults for our boards.
