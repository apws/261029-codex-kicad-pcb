English translation/adaptation of upstream `docs/first-board-checklist.md`. Record PASS, FAIL or UNKNOWN for each item with file location, command result or datasheet page. This catches common mistakes; it is not a complete electrical review.

== Scope and schematic

* [ ] Function, supply and connected equipment are explicit.
* [ ] RF, high-speed, charging or mains portions have appropriate specialist review where relevant.
* [ ] Every IC follows a documented manufacturer circuit and pinout.
* [ ] Each power pin has required decoupling; unused pins follow the datasheet.
* [ ] Voltage ratings and logic levels agree; 3.3 V parts are not assumed 5 V tolerant.
* [ ] LEDs have current limiting; switch/button pull direction agrees with firmware.
* [ ] Reverse-polarity/overcurrent protection is addressed or its omission explicitly accepted.
* [ ] ERC findings and exclusions are explained.

== Parts and footprints

* [ ] Assembly supplier codes, inventory dates and Extended-part costs are recorded.
* [ ] Land patterns match the exact purchased part's dimensions and pins.
* [ ] Pin 1/polarity agrees across symbol, footprint, silkscreen and actual part.
* [ ] Connector pin order, sex and orientation match the mating equipment.

== PCB

* [ ] Board rules satisfy the actual design and selected manufacturing process.
* [ ] Decoupling is close to the corresponding supply pins.
* [ ] Power widths are justified by current/thermal requirements.
* [ ] Return copper is continuous where needed, without unintended islands/splits.
* [ ] Connectors, controls and holes meet enclosure/mating constraints.
* [ ] Board/version/pin labels are legible and clear of pads.
* [ ] DRC, unconnected and parity findings are resolved; exclusions documented.
* [ ] 3D/assembly views show plausible orientation and no unintended interference.

== Files and order

* [ ] Gerber, drill, BOM, CPL and drawings are from one revision/export set.
* [ ] Every Gerber layer and the complete outline have been viewed.
* [ ] JLC BOM/CPL checker passes, or all warnings are explained.
* [ ] DNP/test points/holes/fiducials are filtered appropriately for actual assembly.
* [ ] Submitted files are frozen and referenced by README/WORKLOG.
* [ ] Factory preview polarity, rotation and pin 1 have been checked part by part.
* [ ] Production and placement confirmations retain manual review.
* [ ] Excluded quotation items and additional fees are understood.
* [ ] Uploads, orders, payments, messages and approvals have appropriate user authorization.
