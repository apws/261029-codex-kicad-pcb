== Design terms

Schematic:: Components and electrical connections, stored in `.kicad_sch`.
PCB layout:: Physical placement and copper routing, stored in `.kicad_pcb`.
Symbol:: Schematic representation of a part.
Footprint:: Physical pad/outline definition; match the exact purchased part.
Net:: Electrically connected pins, such as GND or 3V3.
Netlist:: Connectivity listing used to compare intended circuit changes.
ERC:: Electrical Rules Check of schematic connection rules.
DRC:: Design Rules Check of PCB geometry/connectivity against configured rules.
Decoupling:: Local supply capacitance near IC power pins.
Via:: Plated connection between copper layers.
Annular ring:: Copper around a drilled hole.
Zone/pour:: Filled copper area, often ground.
Edge.Cuts:: Finished board outline layer.
Stackup:: Layer materials and thicknesses.
DNP:: Do Not Populate; present in design but omitted from assembly.
Test point:: Accessible measurement pad or connection.

== Manufacturing terms

Gerber:: Layer artwork supplied to the fabricator.
Excellon/drill file:: Hole coordinates and drill sizes.
PTH/NPTH:: Plated/non-plated through holes.
Solder mask:: Protective coating; artwork usually describes openings.
Silkscreen:: Printed labels and markings.
Paste/stencil:: Apertures for applying solder paste.
BOM:: Bill of Materials: references, values, footprints and supplier parts.
CPL:: Component Placement List: position, rotation and side; also pick-and-place file.
Reference/designator:: Identifier such as R1, C3 or U1.
Pin 1:: Orientation reference for a component.
PCB/PCBA:: Bare board / assembled board.
SMT/THT:: Surface mount / through-hole assembly.
MPN:: Manufacturer part number.
C-code:: JLC/LCSC supplier identifier such as C14663.
Basic/Extended:: Assembly inventory categories with different setup costs. The upstream October 2026 observation noted Preferred Extended fee relief in some Economic assembly; check current terms.
Economic/Standard:: JLC assembly tiers with differing side, size and package capabilities.
DFM:: Design for Manufacturing review.
Rails:: Temporary handling strips outside the finished board.
Panel:: Multiple boards processed together.
V-cut:: Scored groove for separating panels.
Fiducial:: Optical alignment mark.
HASL/ENIG/OSP:: Solder leveling / immersion gold / organic solderability protection. Each has assembly and storage tradeoffs.

== Project vocabulary

Fixed release package:: Exact version sent to manufacturing; never silently updated. Avoid calling an arbitrary candidate “final”.
Working copy:: Mutable copy where edits/refill-save are permitted.
Candidate:: Proposed next design, not yet accepted as a release.
Verification:: Pre-release file checks/review, frozen with the release.
Hardware results:: Post-delivery measurements and errata, kept outside the release.
WORKLOG:: Newest-first ownership, changes, verification and stopping-point record.
Writer:: Sole current person/agent/server modifying the design.
PASS:: The particular check completed its declared scope without unresolved findings; not universal electrical approval.
WARNING:: Analysis completed with differences needing review.
INCOMPLETE/ERROR:: Missing/unsupported inputs or execution failure; never a pass.
Production file:: PCB CAM output awaiting approval, distinct from placement confirmation.
Parts placement confirmation:: PCBA part identity, position, angle and polarity review.
Progress inquiry:: Ask for status, owner or next update; no implied acceptance.
Technical clarification:: Ask why or request annotated evidence; does not approve a change.
Accepting factory changes:: Explicit agreement to CAM/design changes, cost, compensation or timing.
Boundary sentence:: Correspondence statement that a question is not production/change/fee approval.
