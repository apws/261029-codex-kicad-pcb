English adaptation of upstream pitfalls reference, September 2026.

== Schedule and money

Advertised review times were not promises; holidays amplified delays. Check holiday banners and include a buffer. An approved/paid order was cancelled with a stated compliance reason, later described by support as staff error, and was not restored. Request written reasons and prepare a replacement plan rather than waiting indefinitely.

Cart totals excluded some depaneling charges until review. Compare the detailed quote and ask about differences. Coupon face value differs from eligible discount; inspect the actual payment amount.

== Support answers

Reassurance such as “sure” or “we are chasing the team” did not identify an owner or update time; chats sometimes closed after roughly two idle minutes. Different staff contradicted whether engineering email had been sent. Check the mailbox first. Refund/compliance questions at the end of long messages were repeatedly ignored; use separate threads.

A reply that the viewer shows the placement centre did not explain the 0.89 mm difference from the original mechanical centre. Follow up on the unresolved discrepancy with an annotated-image request.

== Confirmation and identity

PCBA emails warned of automatic production after 72 hours despite the chosen manual-confirmation setting; requesting manual retention was not evidence that automatic release had been disabled. PCB CAM and PCBA placement require separate review/confirmation.

Saving carts changed IDs; include both old and new privately. A mail connector was once linked to a different account than the order mailbox; verify sender identity before sending.
